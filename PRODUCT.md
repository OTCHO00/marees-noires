# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: the jury and teachers evaluating a school project ("decode/IA"), who launch the game in a desktop browser, judge the first impression of the start menu, then play a few turns. Secondary: anyone the student shows the game to. Desktop with a mouse comes first; phones must stay usable.

## Product Purpose
Marées noires is a turn-based strategy game in the spirit of Polytopia: four rival cults fight over an archipelago that sinks into the sea a little more every few turns. Success: an evaluator understands what the game is within seconds, starts a game without help, and finds it looks professional, never kitsch or amateur.

## Positioning
The rising tide is the mechanism no neighbor has: coasts sink in stages and vanish, so territory shrinks while the cults expand. The world is Lovecraftian and nocturnal: the only warm colors are each cult's light (lantern, flame) in a dark, desaturated sea of fog.

## Operating Context
Runs in the browser (Vite, Three.js). The start menu sits over a live 3D night scene: an island with a lighthouse whose beam sweeps the horizon, abyss-style water, fog banks, a moon. The menu leads to a setup panel (cult, rival count, archipelago size, sandbox and revealed-map options) and a "how to play" panel. Keyboard: Enter starts, Escape goes back.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Four cults: Ordre de la Lanterne, Enfants du Récif, Cercle de Sang, Veilleurs Pâles, each with a color, a light and a sigil.
- Modes: new game, sandbox (unlimited oil and followers), optional revealed map.
- All UI text is French.
- The 3D background scene of the menu is approved and must stay as it is.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Marées noires. Art direction "Lanterne & Brume": dark and desaturated; warmth only comes from the cults' lights.
- Lovecraftian tone, but the user rejected kitsch twice: gothic display type, heavy filled rounded buttons, glowing ornaments (eye motif, green glows) and the overall amateur composition are explicitly unwanted.
- Readability in the spirit of Polytopia is wanted.

## Evidence on Hand
Real game content only: cult names, mottos, city names, rules text (index.html, src/core/data.js). No players, reviews, awards or numbers exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
1. The scene leads; the interface frames it and never competes with it.
2. Readable at a glance: an evaluator knows what to click in two seconds.
3. Dread by restraint: the unsettling tone comes from darkness, silence and precision, not from ornament.
4. Warm light is meaningful: warm color marks a cult or the action, nothing decorative.
