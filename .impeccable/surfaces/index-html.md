# Menu de départ (index.html #startScreen)

Scope: the start menu overlay (main, expedition setup, how to play) over the approved 3D night scene. Mode: Experience. The end screen shares its card styles.

Audience and job: an evaluator opens the game on desktop, must read what it is and start a game in seconds; it must look professional, never kitsch.

Constraints: 3D scene untouched; French copy; Enter starts, Escape goes back; four cults with color and sigil; sandbox and revealed-map options.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ikebana restraint over a night sea: the island and its lighthouse are the one branch, the interface is the quiet alcove around it, and emptiness does the work. Refuses the stacked rounded button column, gothic display type and glowing ornament.

OWN-WORLD: night-fog rail (#0a1416, 88%) with a hairline edge; ink #e7e2d6 (the reference plaster turned to ink), ash #b8b09f for secondary text, hairlines at 22% ink; one lantern-amber accent (#e8a33d, translated from iris blue) only on the primary action and active marks. Display: Bellefair, light, large and airy. Labels and controls: Jost in letter-spaced capitals; body Jost. Rectangular hairline controls, square corners, no shadows, arrows drawn in SVG.

STORY: the visitor sees a calm, uncanny island, reads the name and one line about the sinking archipelago, and starts a new game with the single filled button; sandbox and rules stay one quiet step away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left rail 264px full height: small spaced logotype top, vertical nav with a sliding dot, tide note and version at the foot. Main column from x≈340: "Marées noires" in Bellefair at ~7.5vw over two lines, one sentence of subcopy, then a filled amber "Nouvelle partie →" and an outlined "Bac à sable →". The island holds the right third; a vertical spaced-caps motto with a short hairline sits at the top right edge.

FORM: challenger adopted by the user, growth-metamorphosis-branching-ikebana-ma-arrangement (declined in weighing, chosen on the page), translated from plaster to night; seed:08a82a431e5bce89.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
